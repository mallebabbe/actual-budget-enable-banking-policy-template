# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to a private, non-commercial installation of [Actual Budget](https://actualbudget.org/) used for personal or household financial management.

## Purpose

This application is used exclusively for personal or household budgeting, financial administration, and analysis of financial information.

It is not offered as a financial service to the public or to third parties.

## Banking Data

Bank account information and transaction data may be accessed through [Enable Banking](https://enablebanking.com/) with the explicit authorization of the relevant account holder.

Information accessed may include:

- Bank account information
- Account balances
- Transaction history
- Transaction descriptions and references

This information is used solely for personal or household financial administration and budgeting.

## Data Storage

Financial information is stored within the operator's private Actual Budget installation.

The location and infrastructure used to host Actual Budget depend on the individual operator's configuration.

Access to the Actual Budget installation should be restricted to authorized users.

## Data Sharing

Personal and financial information is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

Information may be processed by technical service providers where necessary to operate the application, host the Actual Budget installation, or establish a banking connection.

This may include Enable Banking, the relevant financial institution, and the hosting provider selected by the operator.

## Bank Access and Authorization

Access to banking information is established through Enable Banking and the relevant financial institution.

The account holder must explicitly authorize access to their bank account information.

Authentication and authorization are handled through the mechanisms provided by the relevant financial institution and Enable Banking.

The Actual Budget operator does not need to collect or store the account holder's online banking password.

## Data Retention

Financial information may be retained within Actual Budget for as long as required by the operator for personal financial administration.

Information can be removed by deleting it from the relevant Actual Budget installation.

Banking authorization may also be revoked using the mechanisms provided by the relevant bank or Enable Banking, where applicable.

## Security

The operator is responsible for securing their Actual Budget installation, hosting environment, login credentials, backups, and other infrastructure under their control.

## Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the processing of information through a particular Actual Budget installation should be directed to the operator of that installation.
